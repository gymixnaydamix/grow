# User Guide

Welcome to EduFlow Pro! This guide will help you navigate the core features of the system.

## Getting Started

### 1. Login
Access the system using your school email and password at the `/login` screen.

### 2. Dashboard
The **Dashboard** provides a real-time overview of your school's health, including active user counts, total revenue, and recent system activity.

## Core Modules

### Platform Core
- **Auth & Roles:** Manage user accounts and assign permissions.
- **Audit Logs:** Track all critical actions taken within the system for security auditing.

### Finance
- **Invoices:** Create and track sent and received invoices. View overdue payments at a glance.
- **Budget:** Monitor school-wide budgets and departmental spending.

### HR & Staff
- **Directory:** A searchable list of all staff members and teachers.
- **Attendance:** Daily tracking of student presence/absence.
- **Leave:** Manage staff leave requests and approvals.

### Admissions
- **Inquiries:** Handle prospective leads from parents.
- **Applicants:** Review and process new student applications.
- **Enrollment:** Convert accepted applicants into full student records.

### Concierge AI
- **Chat:** Ask the AI questions about school policies, schedules, or student data.
- **Documents:** Upload school handbooks or policy documents to train the AI's knowledge base.

## Search & Navigation
Use the **Global Search** bar at the top of the screen to find students, staff, or courses instantly across the entire system.
